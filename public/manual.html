Chapter 1: Introduction

About the System

The Lesson Plan Submission & Review System is a web-based platform designed to streamline the submission, review, and coaching process for lesson plans at Angela Elementary School.

It is fully aligned with DepEd Order No. 016, s. 2026, which mandates the use of the ILAW Framework with embedded Universal Design for Learning (UDL).

Why Was This System Conceptualized? (Rationale)

The system was conceptualized to address several challenges faced by the school:

  • Simplifying the Process: To move away from tedious, paper-based lesson planning and embrace the flexible, efficient approach mandated by DepEd Order No. 016, s. 2026.
  • Ensuring Compliance: To ensure that all submitted lesson plans consistently integrate the ILAW components and UDL principles.
  • Empowering Instructional Leaders: To provide Master Teachers and School Heads with a smart, data-driven coaching tool that generates bilingual recommendations.
  • AI Transparency: To comply with DO 003, s. 2026 by requiring teachers to declare how AI was used.
  • Promoting Positive Discipline: To embed reminders based on DO 40, s. 2012.
  • Data Integrity: To provide a secure, cloud-based repository (MongoDB) for lesson plans.

Chapter 2: Getting Started

System Access

To access the system, open any web browser and go to:

System URL:
https://ilaw-lesson-plan-tracker.onrender.com
Important Note: Because this system is hosted on a free cloud tier, the server "sleeps" after 15 minutes of inactivity. When you open the link, it may take 30 to 50 seconds to load. Please wait patiently.

Chapter 3: For Teachers

Step-by-Step Submission

1 Log In
On the homepage, click the Teacher card. Enter your provided Username and Password.
2 Fill out the Form
Enter the Subject, Grade Level, Term, and Week.
3 Declaration of AI Use
Select how you used Artificial Intelligence in making the plan. (Walang AI, Guided, Limited, o Prohibited).
4 Paste Content
Paste your complete ILAW-compliant lesson plan into the text box.
5 Submit
Click "Submit Plan". You will see it in "My Submissions" with a 🟡 Pending status.

Handling Revisions

If your Instructional Leader finds areas for improvement, the status will change to 🔴 Revision. A button labeled "Edit" will appear. Click it to read the feedback, edit your plan, and click "Resubmit for Review".

Chapter 4: For Instructional Leaders

Step-by-Step Review

1 Log In
On the homepage, click the Instructional Leader card. Enter your name and select your position.
2 Select a Plan
In the list of Pending Submissions, click "Coach & Review".
3 Review the Smart Recommendation
The system will automatically generate an "Overall Coaching & Mentoring Recommendation" based on the ILAW and UDL framework. You can edit this text.
4 Add Concerns
You may add personalized notes in the "Additional Concerns" box.
5 Decide
Click ✅ Approve Plan or 🔴 Request Revision.

Admin / Danger Zone

⚠️ Reset All Submissions: Found at the bottom of the list. Click this only when starting a new School Year to clear all data from the database.

Chapter 5: Troubleshooting & FAQs

1. Bakit "Cannot reach this page" ang lumalabas?
Maaaring natutulog pa ang server. I-refresh lamang ang page at maghintay ng 30-50 segundo.
2. Nakalimutan ko ang aking password. Ano ang gagawin ko?
Makipag-ugnayan sa inyong School Head o IT Coordinator para ma-reset ang inyong password.
3. Pwede ko bang i-delete ang aking submit?
Oo, basta't ang status nito ay 🟡 Pending pa lamang. Kapag na-approve na, hindi na ito pwedeng i-delete upang mapanatili ang record.
4. Paano kung mali ang napindot kong AI Declaration?
Kung Pending pa lamang, i-delete at i-submit muli. Kung na-review na, makipag-usap sa Instructional Leader.
5. Nawala ang internet habang nag-submit, nai-save ba?
Kung hindi lumabas ang "✅ Lesson plan submitted!", hindi ito nai-save. I-check ang "My Submissions" at i-submit muli kung kinakailangan.
